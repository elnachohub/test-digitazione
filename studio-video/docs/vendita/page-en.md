## Sound familiar?

Your clients already pay you for their website, their SEO, maybe their ads.
Now every one of them wants short videos for Reels, TikTok and LinkedIn.

You can make them in-house, one client at a time, and watch a 15-second
clip eat half a day. You can send them to a video studio and wait. Or you
can say no, and hope nobody else says yes.

There's a fourth option: add video to your white-label menu.

---

## How it works

1. **Send us a link.** Your client's website, the one thing the video should
   get people to do ("book a table", "request a quote"), and their logo if
   you have it.
2. **We make the video.** A team of AI agents captures the site, writes the
   script, edits and checks the video. A person reviews every video before
   it reaches you.
3. **You get MP4 files** in 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 and 4:5: ready for YouTube,
   the website, Reels, TikTok, Shorts and feeds. One round of changes is
   included.

---

## What's in the videos

- **Your client's real website**, in a browser or on a phone, scrolling
  from top to bottom. No stock footage, no filming.
- **How-to videos with a moving cursor** that clicks the right parts of the
  page, with zoom and numbered call-outs: "how to book", "how to order",
  "how to sign up".
- **Headline, benefits and a clear call to action**, with the button being
  clicked and the web address easy to read.
- **Four visual styles** (Pop, Editorial, Grid, Gradient), nine colour
  themes, your client's colours and logo.
- **No branding of ours** in the video or the files.
- **Nothing made up.** No invented figures, reviews or claims: only what's
  on your client's site or what they can back up.

What's not in them (yet): voiceover and music. The videos are built to work
with the sound off, the way most people watch in their feed. If you have a
track your client holds the rights to, we'll add it.

## Why agencies use it

- **Fixed price, fixed turnaround.** £59 a video on Agency 10, ~£43 on

  Black Friday push) and we make a version for each of your clients, each

  Spanish, Italian, Portuguese or Dutch, with copy adapted to fit the

## FAQ

**Who actually makes the videos?**
A team of AI agents, each with one job (capture the site, write, edit,
check), and a person who reviews every video before delivery.

**Will my client see your name?**
No. There's no branding of ours in the videos or the files. You deliver
them as your own work.

**What do you need from me?**
The website link, the one action the video should drive, and the logo if
you have it. We take the rest from the site.

**What if the site is behind a login or still being built?**
We need pages we can see. Send us full-page screenshots and we'll start
from those.

**Voiceover? Music?**
Not yet. The videos are designed to be watched on mute. If you have a
licensed track, we'll put it in. Voiceover is on the way; it'll go on this
page when it's ready, not before.

**Can my client approve the video online?**
Not yet: we send you download links and you share the files. An approval
page is on our roadmap.

**What languages do you cover?**
English, plus French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese and Dutch on
request [only the languages already tested by QA]. Copy is adapted to fit
the video, never padded with claims that weren't in the original.

**Who checks the translations?**
We prepare them (AI plus automatic checks). Sign-off on each language sits
with you or your client, often their local distributor. If you want our
guarantee, add native-speaker review.

**Do you add stats or reviews to make the video more persuasive?**
No. We only use what's on the site or what your client can prove.
Invented claims are a risk for you and for them (in the UK, think ASA).

**Who owns the content?**
Your client owns their copy, logo and images; you confirm you're allowed to
use them. [Wording from our legal team.]

**Where are you based? What about support?**
We're based in Italy. Support is in English, in writing, during Italian
business hours (one hour ahead of the UK). [Response time: to be decided.]

**How do I pay?**
By card or bank transfer, in pounds, with a VAT invoice under the reverse
charge. [Payment links and invoice wording: to be prepared.]

**Can I cancel?**
Yes, with 30 days' notice. No annual contract.

**Why not just use Canva or CapCut?**
They're great if you're making the video yourself. We make it for you,
from the website, in every format, for every client. If you need stock
footage, filming or talking avatars, those tools are a better fit.
