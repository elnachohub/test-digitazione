Questions

Do I need AI to get value?

No. The built-in engine answers from your articles, detects language, mood and intent, and runs troubleshooters with zero AI cost. Connect Claude when you want it to handle the long tail and to power agent assist.

What happens when the bot doesn't know?

It says so — it never bluffs. It offers a person, hands over with a summary, and logs the question as a content gap so you can close it for good.

Where does our data live?

On the Tickless server you run — one Node process, your cloud, your region. The demo on this site stores everything in your own browser only.

Which languages are supported?

English, Spanish, French, German, Italian and Portuguese for the widget, bot replies and troubleshooters. With AI connected, answers translate your English articles faithfully.

Can customers see internal notes?

Never. Notes and handoff summaries are filtered on the server before anything reaches a browser, and each visitor can only read their own conversations.

How long does setup take?

Paste the snippet, add your top articles, pick your colors. Most teams are live the same afternoon.
